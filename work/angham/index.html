Black and champagne, for a seated hall.

The Legendary Super Star with her orchestra, for a seated hall at Dubai World Trade Centre. A campaign in black and champagne, from the first promo film to the roll-ups in the hotel partner’s lobby. The event was ours: owned, produced and organized with MCX, and everything managed: the sponsors, the production, the artists, the campaign, the AV.
