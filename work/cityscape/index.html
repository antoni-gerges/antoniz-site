Cityscape Abu Dhabi · Abu Dhabi · 2017

From floor plan to full scale.

Creative direction · Cityscape Abu Dhabi pavilionAbu Dhabi · 2017

A pavilion of 672 square metres for four public bodies and fourteen projects. The brief came as boxes. I answered with one line across the floor: it became the carpet, the route, and a ceiling of light above it.

The idea

The brief came as boxes. I gave it one line.

Four public bodies and fourteen projects on one floor of 672 square metres. One maroon line across the floor would lead to all of them, and become the carpet.

Height

Then we gave it height, and a ceiling of light.

A raised floor of 28 by 24 metres. Two wings up to six metres high, turned around one diagonal. Over the line, spheres hung at different heights, so the ceiling reads as a wave from across the hall.
