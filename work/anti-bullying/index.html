National Bullying Prevention Week · UAE · 2018

Give growth a language.

Creative Director and Producer · National Bullying Prevention WeekAntBrush · UAE · 2018 · for the Ministry of Education

A national week for schools. The mark is two figures with their arms raised and seven leaves growing from their hands. From it grew a line, a world drawn as a hill rising out of a book, a system of posts and things to hold, two stands, and seven days across the country.

The idea

Two figures, seven leaves.

Everything grows from the hands. The mark is two children with their arms up and seven leaves rising from them, in five colours. It began as a hand and became a tree.

The system

Then a line, and a world.

We gave the week a line, Flourish together, and a picture to live in. The film named seven things a school can do.

The week

Then we walked.

Seven days across the country, 22 to 28 April 2018, closing with a walk at La Mer, Dubai.

The full story

Every step, in order.

For the Ministry of Education, UAE, with the Supreme Council for Motherhood and Childhood. Pictures with Arabic lettering are left out.
