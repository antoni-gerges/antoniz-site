Magida El Roumi Live in Dubai · Dubai Media City Amphitheatre · 26 January 2018 · Etisalat presents · with MCX and Catch Me Events

Three rounds of an arch, an evening of light.

Magida El Roumi with her orchestra on the open stage at Dubai Media City, one week after Mounir. The arch went through three rounds; the light was set the evening before. Built and produced for the event’s owner with MCX: the venue from nothing on an open lawn, the stage and its sound, light and screens, the set-up and the logistics; the artist and the event were theirs.
