Dubai Health Authority · Dubai · 2008

A shape with something to say.

Creative direction · Dubai Health Authority identity and launchAntBrush · Dubai · 2008

A new health authority for Dubai. Eight agencies pitched; four were shortlisted. We drew a star with one corner left open, so the authority’s own name completes it. Almost a thousand of its staff voted, and they chose it. Then the launch: first the shape alone, then the colour and the name, across the city.

The idea

An incomplete shape.

Eight agencies pitched; four were shortlisted. We drew twelve routes and made four presentations in three weeks. The star with its open corner went to a vote, and almost a thousand of the authority’s staff chose it.

The launch

First the shape. Then the colour and the name.

The campaign opened with no name at all: only the star, drawn in the picture, in black and white. Then each teaser turned into its revealer.

The full story

Every step, in order.

The vote is on record in Gulf News. The street photographs of the launch carry the bilingual logotype and are shown only in the full story.
