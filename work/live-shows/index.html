Live shows · Dubai · 2017 to 2018 · with MCX

Five nights, one city.

Amr Diab, Angham, Mohamed Mounir, Magida El Roumi, Lilly Singh. Live shows in Dubai, 2017 to 2018: creative direction through AntBrush, with MCX as co-producer. Four of the five were ours: owned, produced and organized with MCX, and everything managed: the sponsors, the production, the artists, the campaign, the AV. For Magida El Roumi, we built and produced the night for the event’s owner.
