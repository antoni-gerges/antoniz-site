Hakawi Sharqia · Ramadan tent · the marquee on the Emirates Golf Club lawn, Dubai · Ramadan 2017 · with MCX

A night venue, built from nothing.

For the month of Ramadan 2017 we built a night venue from nothing on the lawn of Emirates Golf Club: a glass marquee where people broke the day’s fast at sunset and stayed until 3 a.m. We named it, designed it, dressed it, and built a TV studio inside it, where a talk show went out live every night.

The event was ours: owned, produced and organized, with MCX.

Antoni Gerges · Partner, Creative Director & Co-producer, through AntBrush

A red carpet across a golf course.

To a lit marquee on the lawn of Emirates Golf Club: Ramadan nights, sunset to 3 a.m.

Hakawi Sharqia: Eastern tales.

We named it and drew its mark in Arabic calligraphy: a month of evenings told like stories.

It began as a pencil arch.

Drawn by hand inside the empty tent: the studio’s screen, before anything was built.

From the pencil sketch to the gold screen

A pencil line: the arch.

Built from 16 May, open on 27 May: eleven days

The shell, 22 May.

Teal canopies, lanterns and white lounges.

Made for long nights after the fast.

A fretwork counter for every guest.

The same pattern wrapped the buffets.

Inside the tent, a TV studio.

Al Aan TV broadcast a talk show live from here every night at midnight; its own cameras and crew ran the show.

Its mark, and its guests.

We designed the show’s mark too, and booked its guests: actors and singers.

Every night until 3 a.m.

Families, friends and companies, for the whole month; one channel hosted its own staff iftar for 200 here.

With Al Aan TV, Al Arabiya 99 FM, Harlequin, FLOW and Emirates Golf Club.

The club’s kitchens served the food and drink; Al Aan TV ran the cameras of its show; Harlequin put up the marquee; FLOW brought the power and the cooling.

The same marquee came back in July as QD’s 2.0, the summer venue on the same lawn.
