I led the concept and design through AntBrush, working with our team and Halwan Advertising and Production to turn the municipality’s forecourt into an exhibition.

The project

Innovation Month 2024 for Al Ain City Municipality, part of the Department of Municipalities and Transport: two days, 21 and 22 February 2024, from 9:00 am to 2:00 pm, on the forecourt of the municipality’s main building in Al Ain.

I led the creative direction and managed delivery through AntBrush. Our team developed the design; Halwan Advertising and Production carried out the build.

The idea

The national campaign’s mark is a map drawn in squares. I took the square, gave it depth so it became a box, and stacked the boxes into a gate.

The mark belongs to the national campaign. The idea came from it.

Designed, then built

The first proposal, in January 2024, was a gate of boxes under a roof of triangles. The final design, of 2 February 2024, put one big truss over the whole street to carry the shades. That is what was built.

Inside, the walls are folded in a zigzag and carry the campaign’s mark. All the 3D renders are AntBrush’s.

Our presentation of 4 February 2024 set out the venue, the branding and the giveaways: badges, a trophy (the mark as an object built of squares) and a gift box.

Three dates

19 December 2023: nine weeks out, the forecourt was empty. 18 February 2024: three nights before, steel. 21 February 2024: open.

The scope

What we delivered.

The whole job, from the gate to the last prize: the space, the screens, the giveaways and the safety plans.

Space

  • Entrance gate on the month’s theme
  • Fabric shades over the outdoor area
  • 40 booths: 30 for the innovation projects, ten for the municipality’s own
  • Floor carpeting
  • Kids’ corner for ten projects
  • Outdoor flags, six

Screens & content

  • Screens on the booths, ten
  • Robotics and 3D-printing showcase, two days, with staff
  • A registration iPad

Print & gifts

  • Ideas wall with three questions; five quote posters
  • Posters for the municipality’s ten booths
  • Badges with lanyards, 500
  • Trophies, ten
  • Giveaway prizes, 40

Event

  • Method statement, health and safety plan, risk assessment
