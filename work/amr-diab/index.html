Amr Diab Live in Dubai · Dubai Media City Amphitheatre · 29 December 2017 · SWYP presents · with MCX

One look, one night.

The Megastar on the open lawn at Dubai Media City. One look ran from the promo films to the arch, the venue fence, the wristbands and the screens, so the night read as one piece. The event was ours: owned, produced and organized with MCX, and everything managed: the sponsors, the production, the artists, the campaign, the AV.

Antoni Gerges · Partner, Creative Director & Co-producer, through AntBrush
