Akshaya × Vikas Khanna · Dubai · 23 June 2022

A book, and its digital twin.

The launch of “Sacred Foods of India” by chef Vikas Khanna as a physical book with an NFT twin, for Akshaya, Texas. Creative direction, design and the room, through AntBrush, with MCX.

The idea.

One face carries the launch: the book’s reader seen through a headset, the sacred and the digital in a single look.

Drawn, then built.

Each piece went to the fabricators as a production file, and stood in the hotel three weeks after the recce.

The room.

Kinara, JA Resort, Dubai: empty at the recce on 3 June, lit for the night on 23 June.

The object.

The physical half of the launch: the book in a sandalwood box, unveiled in front of the guests.

The night.

23 June 2022, 7:30 pm: the launch, then dinner with the chef.

The film of the night, 23 June 2022

The scope

What we delivered.

The look of the launch and every piece in the room, sent to the fabricators as production files and built with MCX.

Idea & screens

  • The key visual: the VR-goggled head
  • Virtual photo booth: the face, the frames, the QR mobile screen, the microsite background
  • Website link files

Space

  • The VR-head photo wall
  • The stage wall
  • The shield wall: sticker, frosted acrylic, golden frame
  • Podiums
  • Flags and directional signage
  • Venue walls and wood bases

Production

  • Production files for every piece, to the fabricators
  • The build at the hotel, 20 to 23 June 2022, with MCX
