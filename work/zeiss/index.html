The launch

ZEISS Vision Care launched two lenses, ClearView and PhotoFusion X, in one night at The Space, Dubai, on 27 June 2022. I led it from the first sketch to the last screen: the idea, the invitations, the films, the space. Made by AntBrush, with Mission Control X.

The idea

The ZEISS brackets frame what you should look at. So we put the launch inside them: a lens, an eye, and an athlete who is not standard.

The concept board holds the whole idea on one sheet. The invitation carries the campaign athlete and one line: stand out with stock that is not standard. Both photo walls were drawn into the sports hall before anything was printed.

The pieces

The 3D models are made from the production artwork. The way in is one space: the long screen with our film, and ten flags on the carpet. The two signs carry an arrow, the launch name and the logo, printed on both sides. The entrance wall is an L on the stairs, 4 ft 11 in + 11 ft 2 in wide.

The photo booth

My design. Two acrylic lenses, one clear and one tinted, the way PhotoFusion X darkens in the sun. Guests stand behind them. The base is 5 ft 7 in × 2 ft 4 in.

The film for the 74-ft wall runs from IT’S TIME to NOT STANDARD.
