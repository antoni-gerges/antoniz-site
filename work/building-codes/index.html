Abu Dhabi International Building Codes: the launch campaign, Abu Dhabi, 2010–11

Abu Dhabi International Building Codes · 2010–11

Eight codes, one building.

From 1 January 2011, Abu Dhabi implemented international regulations and standards for its construction industry. The launch campaign gave the rules a picture: the names of the codes, stacked into a building.

For the Department of Municipal Affairs, Abu Dhabi (today the Department of Municipalities and Transport), with the municipalities of Abu Dhabi City, Al Ain City and the Western Region. Delivered by AntBrush, from the idea to the launch.

Counting down to 01|01|2011.

Before the launch, a teaser counted down to 01|01|2011. The tower’s floors are the code books, one colour for each code.

Launched at the summit.

The codes went public at the World Future Energy Summit, Abu Dhabi, 17 to 20 January 2011, on the Department’s pavilion in the ADNEC Atrium: green glass, touch kiosks, and the new code books in a glass case. And a hologram presentation of the codes at the 2011 summit, quoted and invoiced.

The photographs survive at 800 and 600 pixels wide, so they are shown no larger.

The pavilion, edition by edition: World Future Energy Summit →

One building, every surface.

The same building runs through print and screen: the user booklet, the folder, the tri-fold brochure, and the website of the Higher Committee of Abu Dhabi Building Codes.

The website: designed by AntBrush and presented this way; built by another company.

Touch a book, open the code.

A touch table: the code-book tower stands on the skyline, the books line the street, and a touch opens each code as a room. Fire: a sprinkler over a living room. Property maintenance: the pipes behind a bathroom wall. Energy conservation: daylight across a façade. By 2015, seven touch presentations were delivered, one for each code.

The look kept going.

  • 2013 · the codes in printThe 2013 edition of the codes: six books, ADIBC, ADIPMC, ADIECC, ADIPSDC, ADIFGC and ADIMC, one colour each, and a user guide in English.
  • 2014 · a poster for each codeApril 2014: one poster per code, signed by the Department and the three municipalities.
  • 2015 · at the summitJanuary 2015: six codes artworks, seven touch presentations and seven films for the Department’s stand.
