Why Not! Mazzika and the concerts · Dubai · 2017–19

Make room for six voices.

Partner and co-producer · Why Not! Mazzika campaign and visual systemAntBrush · Dubai · 2019 · with Mission Control X

Six Egyptian bands, one night at the Coca-Cola Arena. I built a system where any act could step into the same frame: a square with its corners cut, black on gold. Six frames settled into one key visual, and on the night the frame held the live picture on the arena’s screens. Before it, three concerts in Dubai under one title line.

The idea

One square, from a letter to an arena.

The O is a square. The badge is a square with its corners cut. I built the system so any act could step into the same frame, and six frames settle into one key visual.

The night

The frame held the live picture.

Coca-Cola Arena, Dubai, 22 November 2019. On the night, the act frame ran on the arena’s side screens.

Before it: the concerts

Drawn, then there.

Angham, Amr Diab and Mohamed Mounir, Live in Dubai, November 2017 to January 2018. One thin title frame carried all three nights. Partner and co-producer, with Mission Control X.
