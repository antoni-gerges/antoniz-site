The festival

A whole weekend of food and music, captured in one drawing.

Abu Dhabi Food Festival 2018 · opening weekend6 to 8 December · Yas Links, Yas Island, Abu Dhabi
Read more

The Big Grill was one of the two events of Abu Dhabi Food Festival 2018, on its opening weekend. I designed the poster and led the creative direction through AntBrush; with MCX, we brought that visual world into the festival at Yas Links. The event was ours: owned, produced and organized with MCX, and everything managed: the sponsors, the production, the artists, the campaign, the AV.

The festival

The Big Grill Festival, 6 to 8 December 2018, on the lawn of the Yas Links golf club on Yas Island, Abu Dhabi. A family festival, free to all, from noon to 11 pm, with a kids’ zone on the lawn. Organised by MCX and presented by SWYP.

Its theme was “Grillin’ ’n’ Chillin’”: restaurants and steakhouses at the grills, and music on the lawn. Big Mountain, Abbamania, Abri & Funk Radius and KIZA played.

My part

As Partner, Creative Director & Co-producer at MCX, through AntBrush, I helped originate and deliver the festival. I designed two poster routes; the team carried the visual system into the event.

The Big Grill logo belongs to the festival.

The idea

The poster’s own line: “Flamin’ grills + smokin’ tunes = ☺”. Food and music as one weekend, said in one drawing: a guitarist for the tunes, a grill with its smoke, a classic car for the cars on show, a golf ball on its tee for the links, and the city behind.

Everything in the drawing runs on one slant: the date, the shadows, the stripe.

Two routes

The first is painted: a sky from teal to ochre, palms and a skyline. The second is cut harder: three wedges and a stripe on a pale ground, inside a frame drawn by hand. It went out in red and in ochre, tall and square.

The same wedge, car and grill stood on the boards at the way in.

The files

Both routes are my Illustrator files of 18 July 2018. The exported posters add a tagline and a place line that the working files do not have.

The 3D

The lawn in 3D is a model made for this portfolio, from my photographs of 6 December 2018 and the festival film. Sizes are estimates. The stalls, the kids’ zone and the beanbags are placed as the film shows them. The lit beam across the top of the three X’s is added in the model; the stage as built had three free-standing X’s.

The films and the photograph

The festival film is by MCX, from its own channel. The film and the photograph of the stage on the first night are my own, taken on my phone on 6 December 2018.

The scope

What we delivered.

Ours: owned, produced and organized, with MCX

  • Building the whole venue from nothing on the open ground: Stage X and its screens, the grills, the tents and pergolas, the lawn set-up
  • The stage, sound, light and screens: AV production
  • The set-up and the logistics
  • Ticketing and sponsorship
  • The running order and the days themselves

Campaign & print

  • Key visual and posters, two routes, hand-drawn
  • For SWYP: bridge banners on the Yas highway and towards Salam Street
  • Foam boards and a billboard at Yas Links

Digital & radio

  • Social and paid digital campaign, with a competition
  • Ticketing on Platinumlist; vendor recruitment
  • SMS in two phases; radio, 25 November to 8 December

Space

  • Stage X and the festival build
