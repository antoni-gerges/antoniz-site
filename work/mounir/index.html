Mohamed Mounir Live in Dubai · Dubai Media City Amphitheatre · 19 January 2018 · SWYP presents · with MCX

One key visual, rebuilt for every place.

The Legendary Mohamed Mounir, his first Dubai concert in four years, with Sharmoofers opening, inside Dubai Shopping Festival. The event was ours: owned, produced and organized with MCX, and everything managed: the sponsors, the production, the artists, the campaign, the AV.
