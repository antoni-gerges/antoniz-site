The association

AIAASC is the American International Accreditation Association of Schools and Colleges, in New York. It accredits schools and colleges outside the United States. Its mission in our proposal: “To promote improvement for international schools through American accreditation system.”

I rebranded it and ran its brand with full responsibility from Dubai, through AntBrush.

The plan

The plan gave the association a clearer position and a three-stage path: establish the identity, reach its audiences, then build lasting relationships.

It named seven markets first (the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Qatar, Egypt and North Africa), four audiences (ministries of education, teachers and their associations, students and parents, partners) and three phases: rebrand and position, from January 2021; outreach, from April 2021; loyalty, from the new academic year in September 2021.

The idea of the mark

From our presentation of 26 March 2021: A is for Alpha, the first. The star is excellence. The red line is a shooting star, “reaching the stars”. And blue and red, “the colors of the American flag to capture the reputation and quality of the American Education.”

The mark that read AIAA now reads AIAASC, S and C in red.

The system and the campaigns

The brand toolkit of 18 August 2021 sets out the mark and its icon, colours and type (Ivy Mode and Gotham), stationery, print and advertising formats, roll-ups, banners, signs and gifts.

Back to school, August and September 2021: eight posts and a film, each beginning “It’s time”. Two films on what accreditation gives, for colleges and for schools. A film for World Teachers’ Day, October 2021.

The 3D models are made for this portfolio from the delivered logo, the toolkit and the campaign artwork; the flag is drawn for the model.
