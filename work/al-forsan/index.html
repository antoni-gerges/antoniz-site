The project

A resort in Abu Dhabi for five sports, named for the knights. I gave it its mark, its system and its campaigns.

2008 to 2013Al Forsan International Sports Resort, Abu Dhabi
Read more
The resort

Al Forsan means the knights. Al Forsan International Sports Resort put riding, motor sport, shooting, paintball and water sport on one site in Abu Dhabi.

It belongs to PPM, Private Property Management. The identity was approved between December 2008 and March 2009. I led it through AntBrush, my agency.

The idea

The manual names three parts. The arch: classic Arab architecture and the peak of a tent; the white space inside it is a knight’s helmet. The wave: the dunes. The knight: a rider with his head cloth and his rifle, coming through the arch.

The line under it all: Where knights rise.

The choice

The first manual, of December 2008, carried an earlier logo without the arch. In March 2009 we presented six arches around the same knight; the mark took the gate.

The system

The identity manual has 136 pages: the mark and its grid, colours and type, stationery, advertising and signs. Its colours are one gold and one black.

Five sport centres carry the same mark, each with a line in its own colour: Equestrian, Paintball, Motor Sports, Water Sports, Shooting.

The campaigns

The soft launch put every sport in one picture. The launch campaign, Your gateway to awesomeness, built the arch of the mark as a gate with every sport coming through it.

Hoardings stood around the site while the resort was being built, and the brand stood at an exhibition. The work for the resort ran from 2008 to 2013. PPM came back for its own logo, and for the names and signs of the Conrad Dubai.

This page

The interactive 3D models are made for this portfolio, from the manual’s own drawing of the logo. The interactive models use the primary black horse and lettering with the gold gate and dunes.
