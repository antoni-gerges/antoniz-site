  3. The name

    The mark had four letters. The name had six.

    It worked under a mark that read AIAA, while its name already ended in Schools and Colleges. We gave the mark its last two letters.

II

The mark

Three ideas in one letter: the first, a star, and the colours of a flag.

  1. The idea

    A is for Alpha. We sent a star through it.

    The first letter, for the first in its field. A star for excellence. A shooting star to carry it upward.

    The mark’s own letter in 3D · portfolio presentation

    Three concept plates from our presentation of 26 March 2021.

  2. The colours

    Blue and red, from the American flag.

    Blue for trust. Red for energy. Together they carry the standing of American education, which is what the association takes abroad.

  3. The mark

    A rising line, a star, and the two missing letters.

    Four letters in blue. A red line that rises through the A to a star. Then S and C, and the whole name beneath.

    The delivered vectors, animated for this portfolio

  4. The system

    Seventy-four pages behind it.

    The brand toolkit: voice, type, colour, the icon, advertising, signage. Set in Ivy Mode and Gotham.

    AIAASC Guidelines, August 2021. Fifty-three of its 74 pages pass here.

The record

For AIAASC, I led the repositioning, the change of the mark from AIAA to AIAASC, the identity that carries it, and a year of campaigns under it. The client was in New York. I led the work from Dubai.

For
AIAASC, the American International Accreditation Association of Schools and Colleges, New York. It accredits schools and colleges outside the United States.
When
Proposal, November 2020. Agreement signed, January 2021. Presentations, 20 February to 27 March 2021. First phase completed, 25 June 2021. Launch set for 4 July 2021. Toolkit, August 2021. Logo file, 20 October 2021. Films sent, 2 January 2022.
Where
Led from Dubai. We worked at a distance, with a weekly meeting on Mondays at five, Dubai time.
My part
I directed the work and was on every exchange with the client, through AntBrush, my studio.
With
The AntBrush team: marketing by Nashwa Nader and Rahma Himid; design with Nevin Nawar, senior art director. Who animated the films is not recorded.
Made
A marketing plan in three phases; the mark and its icon; a brand toolkit of 74 pages; stationery; a certificate template; a website design; page covers; a press release in English and Arabic; the back-to-school campaign and its film; two films on the benefits of accreditation; a responsibility strategy; a sales kit, brochure and leaflet.
Proposed
A child-protection training programme, to the UAE Ministry of Education, April 2021. Its outcome is not on file.
In their words
The association’s announcement of July 2021 credits “the efforts of AntBrush Founder, Antoni Gerges”.
Not claimed
Any result of a campaign, that the website is live today, or that a certificate was issued. None was checked.
