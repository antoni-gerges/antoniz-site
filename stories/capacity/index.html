2018

Etisalat · Capacity Middle East gala dinner · 7 March 2018

The brief said Armani Hotel. The room was a terrace under the Burj Khalifa.

Won by tender on 25 February. Ten days to the doors.

2018 · Directions

I drew two directions.

One stage in soft curves, one built from tiles of light. Then we stood the second one on the terrace photograph, to see it against the city.

Design renders, 2018

2018 · The screens

Then the city became one line.

On the side screens it draws in from the left and from the right. On the main screen it becomes the skyline under the title.

3D: a portfolio presentation of the screen films

2018 · The evening

A film for every moment of the night.

Welcome, the fountain, the mentalist, dinner, the raffle, the close. Nine films, cut to the running order.

2019

Etisalat · Capacity Middle East gala dinner · 6 March 2019

A year later, Etisalat came back. This time, the beach at Atlantis.

Open air, sand, no walls.

2019 · The room

With nothing to build on, the light became the room.

A lit walkway, drummers carrying light, the stage over the water.

With Mission Control X · for Etisalat · Nasimi Beach, 6 March 2019

From the floor, that night.

Two editions, two rooms, one client who came back.
