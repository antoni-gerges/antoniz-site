ZEISS · Dubai · 2022

ZEISS Vision Care · The Space, Dubai · 27 June 2022

It started as three drawings on one sheet.

A lens, the ZEISS brackets, an eye. Two new lenses to launch to the region's opticians.

3D: a portfolio presentation of my concept board

The idea

Through the lens, the eye comes clear.

A lens earns its place by what you see through it. So the lens had to make the eye larger and sharper, never hide it.

Two lenses, one picture

In the light, it darkens. Out of it, it clears.

ClearView for clarity; PhotoFusion X tints in sunlight. The same picture could carry both.

The key visual

Three things, one picture.

Front-on, the model gives way to the artwork itself.

Original artwork, AntBrush, 2022

The system

Then the brackets travelled.

Around the date, the eye, the invitation, the flags and the signs. One frame, everywhere a guest looked.

Designed to built

One wall, three dates.

Measured on 17 June. Drawn by the 25th. Built for the 27th, with the eye as a disc of light and the plinths in front.

Creative Director and Producer · The Space, Dubai · 27 June 2022 · with Mission Control X

Then the guests came through the brackets.

A dinner among the cars, the keynote under "Seeing beyond", the team at the photo wall.
