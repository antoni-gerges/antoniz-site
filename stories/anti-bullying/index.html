II

The line and the world

A mark is not a campaign. It needed words, a place to live, and a way to teach.

  1. The line

    We gave it a line: Flourish together.

    It went on everything, in two languages. Frames from the campaign’s own film.

    Animation: Mostafa Khamis

  2. The key visual

    Then a world: a hill growing out of an open book.

    A tree, a kite, families, a small sign that says Anti Bullying City. It went on the posters, the brochure, the flags and the screens.

  3. The film

    The film names seven things a school can do.

    One wheel, seven parts, in Arabic and in English. We wrote it, designed it and had it animated.

  4. Print

    Ten thousand brochures, in two languages.

    I led the writing as well as the design, in Arabic and English. A thousand posters went to schools in six emirates. Two of the English pages.

I The mark 1 / 17

The record

I led the identity and the campaign for the UAE’s first National Bullying Prevention Week. We were confirmed on 18 April 2018. The week opened on the 22nd.

For
Ministry of Education, United Arab Emirates, with the Supreme Council for Motherhood and Childhood. Under the patronage of H.H. Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak.
When
22 to 28 April 2018
Where
Schools and malls across the emirates. Filmed at Dubai School for Secondary Education for Boys, Dalma Mall in Abu Dhabi, RAK Mall in Ras Al Khaimah, and La Mer in Dubai. These are venues, not clients.
My part
Creative Director and Producer, through AntBrush, my studio: the mark, the line, the key visual, the lessons, the film, the stands and the closing walk.
With
The AntBrush design team, with Nevin Nawar as senior art director. Mostafa Khamis, who animated the film. UMI, who built the stands. Meraas, La Mer’s developer, for the walk.
Made
Mark and guideline, key visual, 1,000 posters, 10,000 brochures written in Arabic and English, 45 social posts, Snapchat geofilters, 1,000 T-shirts, 3,000 pins, 5,000 wristbands, 200 scarves, the film in Arabic and English, three stands (two of them sited), 14 standing cut-outs, a youth circle, daily film coverage, the closing walk.
After
We made the first week. The third ran in November 2019 as “Together we flourish with tolerance”, and the Ministry held its sixth in November 2022. The later weeks are not our work.

Sources: the delivered logo file; the Ministry’s release of 22 April 2018; our final report to the Ministry, 16 July 2018; the five dated coverage films; camera dates in the photographs; WAM, November 2019; Al Ittihad, 13 November 2022. Private preview. Nothing here is published.
