Cityscape Abu Dhabi · 2017

Cityscape Abu Dhabi · ADNEC, Hall 8 · 18–20 April 2017

The brief came as boxes.

Four public bodies, fourteen projects, one floor of 672 square metres.

The system

I gave it one line.

Every project got its own name plate. One maroon line would lead to all of them.

Names and pattern: original artwork, April 2017

The plan, in depth

We drew it on the floor, then gave it height.

A raised floor, 28 metres by 24. Two wings up to six metres high, turned around one diagonal.

3D model: a portfolio presentation, built on the design plan

The design

Over the line, a ceiling of light.

Spheres hung at different heights, so the ceiling reads as a wave from across the hall.

Fabrication

We built it in the hall.

Hall 8 opened to us at 2 pm on 14 April. The stand was due on the 17th.

Creative Director and Producer · ADNEC, Hall 8 · 18 April 2017

Then the doors opened.

The real carpet, the real tower, the real ceiling. Frames from our show film.

Strike

Three days later, we took it down.

What stays is the drawing, and one line across the floor.

The record

Client
Department of Municipal Affairs and Transport, Abu Dhabi, with the Abu Dhabi City, Al Ain City and Al Dhafra Region municipalities.
My part
Creative Director and Producer, through AntBrush, my studio: the idea, the stand design, the graphics, the show film, the suppliers, the venue approvals, the build, the show and the strike.
Place
Cityscape Abu Dhabi, Abu Dhabi National Exhibition Centre, Hall 8, stand 8D20. 18–20 April 2017.Hall access from 14 April, 2 pm.
Size
672 m²: a raised floor of 28 × 24 metres with two mezzanines.
Built by
Divine Creations L.L.C, Dubai: fabrication, installation and dismantling, to AntBrush's design. The fabrication drawings are theirs.
Organiser
Informa Exhibitions. The organiser and the venue are where the work happened, not who it was for.
Film
Show film, 2:38, AntBrush. Shown muted.
To note
The developments shown as scale models are the client's projects. The 3D model on this page is a portfolio presentation; the renders are the design as presented in March 2017.
