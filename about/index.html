I’m Antoni Gerges, Creative Director, Brand & Experiential. Tony, once we’ve met. I lead ideas from the first drawing to the real thing.

I trained in graphic design at the Faculty of Fine Arts in Minia, Egypt, and began my career in Cairo in 2003. Dubai followed in 2006.

I founded AntBrush in 2007 and led a team of up to 15 across creative, design, 3D, marketing, account management and accounts. Working across Dubai and Abu Dhabi, we developed identities, campaigns and experiences for public bodies, brands and audiences. My role was to set the creative direction, support the team and answer for the client and the result.

The work ranged from Dubai Health Authority’s identity to ZEISS’s launch and, with my partners at MCX, live shows for artists including Amr Diab and Lilly Singh. Each called for a clear idea and people with different skills working toward it.

Since December 2024, Tampa has been home. Today, 3D, motion and AI are part of how I explore and make work. The tools keep changing; the responsibility for the idea and the result stays with me.

Agency experienceClose

Seventeen years of agency work across Dubai and Abu Dhabi brought together creative teams, specialist makers and production partners. I led the work from the brief through delivery, staying close to both the people making it and the client trusting us with it.

From 2017 to 2022 I was also Partner, Creative Director & Co-producer of MCX in Dubai, producing live shows for Amr Diab, Angham, Mohamed Mounir, Magida El Roumi and Lilly Singh, and festivals and launches for Etisalat, Unilever, ZEISS and Publicis.
