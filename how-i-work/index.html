How I work

Three moves.

The same three, whether the job is a mark, a poster or a room. Each is shown twice, with real work from two projects.

01

Find the one idea.

Every brief hides one idea.I look until I find it.Then everything else is built from it.One picture, not ten.

02

Draw many. Choose one.

I draw more than one route.I put them side by side.I choose the one that holds.Then I can tell you why.

03

Stay until it stands.

I stay close to the work.I check the plan against the room.I work with the team as it takes shape.I’m there when the doors open.
